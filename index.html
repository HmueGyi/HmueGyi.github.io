About Me

I'm Hmue Set San, a Robotics & AI Developer who builds systems that see, listen and act — computer-vision models, local LLM agents and ROS 2 robots that run on real hardware, not just in notebooks.

I studied computer science across three universities — Computer University (Sittwe), Computer University (Monywa) and Computer University (Pathein) — which gave me first-hand experience of very different lab setups, teams and problem-solving styles.

Today I design and ship intelligent robotic solutions: perception pipelines, motion and automation, and offline-first AI so a system keeps working when the network does not.
